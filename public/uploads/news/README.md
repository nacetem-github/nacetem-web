# News Uploads

Use these folders for news entry materials:

- `source-documents/<year>/<article-slug>/` for Word documents and source copy
- `images/<year>/<article-slug>/` for cover images and supporting article photos

Example:

`public/uploads/news/source-documents/2026/nacetem-example-news/story.docx`

`public/uploads/news/images/2026/nacetem-example-news/cover.jpg`

Use `images/` for news article images.
